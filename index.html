Yimu Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Yimu (the "App") is a subscription tracker that runs locally on your phone. Our principle is simple: no account to sign up for, no server of our own, and your subscription data stays on your phone. This policy explains what information the App touches, why, where it is stored, and how you can manage it.

1. What we don't collect

The App does not require you to sign up or log in, and does not collect identity information such as your name, phone number, email address or ID number.

The App has no server of its own and does not upload your subscription data, screenshots, bills or notification content to us. We cannot see any of this data.

The App contains no ads, uses no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, does no user profiling, and does not sell or share your personal information.

2. Data stored on your phone

The subscriptions you add (name, amount, currency, billing cycle and dates, reminder settings, category, payment method, notes, cancellation link, names of family members you split with, etc.) and the App's settings are stored in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Automatic backup files are stored in the App's own folder on your phone (Android/data/…/files/backups), keeping at most the 10 most recent. The system deletes these files when you uninstall the App.

Photos you choose as custom icons are copied into the App's private storage.

3. How each feature uses information

Screenshot import: screenshots you choose are recognized on your phone only, using Google ML Kit's offline text recognition model. The recognized text is used to find subscription names, amounts and dates. The screenshots themselves are not uploaded and are not saved separately by the App.

Bill import: Alipay / WeChat bill files you choose are read on your phone only, to find charges that repeat on a cycle. The file contents are discarded once read; only the subscriptions you confirm are saved, and no other spending in the bill is recorded. An archive password is used only for that one extraction and is not saved.

Subscription discovery (optional, off by default): works only after you turn it on in Settings and grant the App "Notification access" in system settings. Once on, the App processes only notifications from Alipay, WeChat, Google Play, certain Chinese banking apps, and widely used overseas wallet and banking apps (such as PayPal, Google Wallet, Samsung Wallet, Revolut, Monzo, Wise and Chase; the exact list is built into the App), and keeps only notifications that look like payments (title, text and time), to find recurring charges and suggest them on the home screen. These records are stored only on your phone, at most 200 of them, and are deleted automatically after 400 days. The App does not read text messages and does not process notifications from other apps. You can turn this off at any time in the App's settings or in system settings.

Family split: members are just names you enter. The App never contacts them, and they don't need to install the App. "Remind to pay" opens the system share sheet, and you choose who to send the message to.

Sharing your subscription list, exporting CSV, and backing up to a file all go through the system share sheet, where you choose where to save or send.

Phone AI assistants and shortcuts: if you let an AI assistant on your phone (for example, one that reads the screen and taps through accessibility services) or a shortcut operate the App, it can see the subscription details shown on screen at that time. That is your authorization to that assistant, and its provider’s privacy policy applies to how it handles the information; the App never sends data to it on its own. The App offers yimu:// links so AI assistants and shortcuts can add subscriptions or mark bills paid directly. Whatever comes in through a link is stored only on your phone, and additions can be undone.

Mu, the in-app assistant (optional): what you say to Mu is processed on your phone only, by the phone’s built-in Gemini Nano (through Android’s AICore system service) or by a model you choose to download. Your words and subscription data are not sent to us or to any server, and chats are not saved. Changes it proposes take effect only after you confirm them.

4. When the App goes online

Exchange rates: at most once a day, the App requests public currency exchange rates from open.er-api.com to convert totals into your main currency. The request contains none of your subscription data. As with any network request, that server sees your IP address.

Pro purchase: completed through Google Play Billing. Payment information is handled by Google; the App cannot access your bank card or account information and only receives whether you have purchased. Google's privacy policy applies to Google's handling of that data.

Text recognition component: Google ML Kit may contact Google servers from time to time to get bug fixes, model updates and hardware compatibility information, and to send metrics about the component's performance and usage. According to Google, this data does not include the images or text you recognize.

Assistant model download (only if you choose to download one): the model file comes from ModelScope (modelscope.cn) or Hugging Face (huggingface.co). The request contains none of your data; as with any download, those servers see your IP address. The built-in Gemini Nano is downloaded and updated by the system (AICore), and Google’s privacy policy applies to it.

5. System permissions

Notifications: used to remind you before charges and before trials end. Requested only after you add your first subscription; if you decline, everything else still works.

Run at startup: re-schedules reminders after your phone restarts, so none are missed.

Network: used only for the purposes listed in section 4.

Notification access (optional): used only for "Subscription discovery"; see section 3.

Photos and files: the App does not request permission to read all your photos or files. It only reads the images or the single file you pick through the system picker.

6. Your control

View and edit: all data can be viewed and edited directly in the App.

Delete: you can delete individual subscriptions. Uninstalling the App deletes all of the App's data on your phone (except backup files you saved elsewhere yourself). We keep no server copy, so deleted data cannot be recovered from us.

Export: you can export a CSV or backup file at any time under "Settings › Data".

Withdraw permission: notifications and notification access can be turned off at any time in system settings.

7. Minors

The App is meant for adults managing their own subscription spending and is not directed at children under 14.

8. Policy updates

If the way the App handles data changes, we will update this policy and its effective date. For significant changes that involve collecting or uploading new data, we will clearly notify you in the App and ask for your consent.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us through the developer contact details on the app store listing.

一目订阅 隐私政策

生效日期：2026年11月22日

一目订阅（以下简称「本 App」）是一款在手机本地运行的订阅管理工具。我们的原则很简单：不需要注册账号，没有自己的服务器，你的订阅数据只保存在你的手机上。这份政策说明本 App 会接触哪些信息、为什么、存在哪里，以及你可以怎么管理。

一、我们不收集什么

本 App 不要求注册或登录，不收集你的姓名、手机号、邮箱、身份证号等身份信息。

本 App 没有自己的服务器，不会把你的订阅数据、截图、账单或通知内容上传到我们这里。我们也看不到这些数据。

本 App 不含广告，不接入第三方统计或广告 SDK，不做用户画像，不出售或共享你的个人信息。

二、保存在你手机上的数据

你添加的订阅（名称、金额、币种、扣费周期和日期、提醒设置、分类、付款方式、备注、退订链接、和家人分摊的成员名字等）和 App 设置，保存在本 App 的私有存储空间里，其他 App 无法读取。

自动备份文件保存在手机中本 App 专属的目录（Android/data/…/files/backups），最多保留最近 10 份。卸载本 App 时这些文件会被系统一起删除。

你选择的自定义图标照片会复制一份到本 App 的私有存储空间。

三、各项功能如何使用信息

截图导入：你主动选择的截图只在手机上用 Google ML Kit 的离线文字识别模型识别，识别出的文字用来找出订阅名称、金额和日期。截图本身不会上传，也不会被本 App 另外保存。

账单导入：你主动选择的支付宝 / 微信账单文件只在手机上读取，用来找出按周期重复的扣款。读完后文件内容即被丢弃，只有你确认导入的订阅会被保存，账单里的其他消费一概不记录。压缩包密码只用于当次解压，不会保存。

自动发现订阅（可选，默认关闭）：只有你在设置里开启，并在系统设置中授予本 App「通知使用权」后才会工作。开启后，本 App 只处理支付宝、微信、Google Play、部分国内银行 App，以及海外常用的钱包和银行 App（如 PayPal、Google 钱包、Samsung Wallet、Revolut、Monzo、Wise、Chase 等，具体以 App 内名单为准）发出的通知，并且只保留看起来是付款的通知（标题、正文和时间），用来找出周期性扣款并在首页提示你。这些记录只保存在手机上，最多 200 条，超过 400 天自动删除。本 App 不读取短信，也不处理其他 App 的通知。你可以随时在本 App 设置或系统设置中关闭。

和家人分摊：成员只是你输入的名字，不会联系对方，也不需要对方安装本 App。「提醒付款」会打开系统分享面板，消息由你自己选择发给谁。

分享订阅清单、导出 CSV、备份到文件：都通过系统分享面板由你自己选择保存或发送的位置。

手机 AI 助手与快捷指令：如果你让手机上的 AI 助手（例如通过无障碍服务读取屏幕、模拟点击）或快捷指令来操作本 App，它们能看到当时屏幕上显示的订阅内容。这是你对该 AI 助手的授权，相关信息如何处理适用其提供方的隐私政策；本 App 不会主动把数据发给它们。本 App 提供 yimu:// 链接入口，供 AI 助手和快捷指令直接添加订阅或标记账单已缴，通过链接传入的内容只保存在你的手机上，添加后可以撤销。

小目（App 内助手，可选）：你对小目说的话只在手机上处理，用的是手机自带的 Gemini Nano（通过安卓系统的 AICore 服务），或者你自己选择下载的模型。你说的话和订阅数据不会发给我们或任何服务器，对话也不会保存。它提出的修改要你确认后才会生效。

四、需要联网的地方

汇率：本 App 每天最多一次向 open.er-api.com 请求公开的货币汇率，用来把总额换算成你的主货币。请求中不包含你的任何订阅数据。和所有网络请求一样，对方服务器会看到你的网络 IP 地址。

Pro 购买：通过 Google Play 结算完成，付款信息由 Google 处理，本 App 拿不到你的银行卡或账户信息，只会得到「是否已购买」的结果。Google 对相关数据的处理适用 Google 的隐私政策。

文字识别组件：Google ML Kit 可能会不定期连接 Google 服务器，获取问题修复、模型更新和硬件兼容信息，并发送关于该组件性能和使用情况的统计数据。根据 Google 的说明，这些数据不包含你识别的图片或文字内容。

小目模型下载（仅在你选择下载时）：模型文件从魔搭社区（modelscope.cn）或 Hugging Face（huggingface.co）下载，请求里不包含你的任何数据；和所有下载一样，对方服务器能看到你的 IP 地址。手机自带的 Gemini Nano 由系统（AICore）下载和更新，适用 Google 的隐私政策。

五、系统权限说明

通知：用于在扣费前和试用结束前提醒你。第一次添加订阅后才会请求，拒绝后其他功能照常使用。

开机启动：手机重启后重新安排提醒，避免漏提醒。

网络：仅用于上面第四部分列出的用途。

通知使用权（可选）：仅用于「自动发现订阅」，见第三部分。

照片和文件：本 App 不申请读取全部照片或文件的权限，只通过系统选择器读取你主动选中的那几张图片或那一个文件。

六、你的控制权

查看和修改：所有数据都可以在本 App 里直接查看和编辑。

删除：可以删除单个订阅；卸载本 App 会删除本 App 在手机上的全部数据（你自己另存到其他位置的备份文件除外）。我们没有服务器副本，所以删除后无法从我们这里找回。

导出：可以随时在「设置 › 数据」中导出 CSV 或备份文件。

撤回授权：通知、通知使用权都可以在系统设置中随时关闭。

七、未成年人

本 App 面向成年人管理自己的订阅支出，不面向 14 周岁以下的儿童。

八、政策更新

如果本 App 处理数据的方式发生变化，我们会更新这份政策并修改生效日期；涉及新增收集或上传数据的重大变化，会在 App 内明确提示并征得你的同意。

联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，可以通过应用商店页面上的开发者联系方式联系我们。
